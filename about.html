About

A kitchen, a tray of sticks, and one late-night promise

MerriMade started the way these things usually do — one birthday, forty cake pops, and a promise made at half past ten the night before. They went in a shoebox lined with tissue paper. Two parents asked for the number before the candles were out.

There is no shop. There is a domestic kitchen, a marble slab for tempering, and a hard limit on how many trays fit in one weekend. That limit is the whole point: nothing here is scaled up to the edge of what's possible. Cake gets baked and crumbled Wednesday. Dipping happens Thursday. Custom designs get a conversation first.

A cake pop is mostly patience. You can taste when someone rushed it.

Where things come from

Chocolate is proper couverture, tempered by hand rather than melted in a bowl and hoped for — it's the difference between a shell that snaps and one that goes waxy on the roof of your mouth. Cakes are baked here, not bought in. Eggs and cream come from the farm at the end of Rosewood Lane. Sprinkles are the one thing shipped in from far away, and there are far too many jars of them.

What you should know

This is a home kitchen, not a registered commercial premises — worth saying plainly. Wheat, egg, dairy, soy and nuts are all handled on the same surfaces, so nothing can be promised as free from any of them. Ask about a specific treat and you'll get a straight answer about what's in it.

Ordering

How to place an order

No cart, no checkout — just a message. It's a small operation and this is genuinely the fastest way.

Tell us about the party

Email or phone with the date, the numbers, and the colours if you have them. A photo of the invitation or a picture you've saved says more than a paragraph ever will. Mention anything we should know — an allergy in the room, a theme, a favourite flavour.

We quote, and sketch

You'll get a reply within a day with a total and a collection slot. Custom designs get a mock-up to approve before anything is dipped, and a deposit once you're happy with it.

Collect or get it delivered

Rosewood Lane, Saturday between ten and two — boxed, ribboned and ready to hand round. If Saturday never works, say so: Sunday delivery within ten miles is an option.
